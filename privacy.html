Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

FaithStudio is a private tool operated by a single creator to publish their own videos to their own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok).

What we access

With the account owner's permission (OAuth), FaithStudio accesses only the owner's own accounts in order to: upload videos, read basic profile information, read view and engagement statistics, and read and reply to comments on the owner's own videos.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users.
  • We do not use data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access any account other than the owner's.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are never sent to any third party. Comment text may be processed by an AI model running locally on that computer to draft replies; it is not sent to outside services.

Deletion

The owner can revoke FaithStudio's access at any time from the security settings of Google, Meta or TikTok. Locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact the owner via github.com/hari-94.
